Diary of the mission among the Hottentots near the Cape of Good Hope, from November 1796 to 19 May 97.

On 5 Nov. 1796 the overseer of the warm bath paid us a visit with a party from the Cape, and shortly after Mr Wolfrum arrived with a second party, also from there [3]. These two together made up a number of 16 persons, who stayed the night with us and attended the Sunday meetings the next day; on which occasion they wondered greatly at the quiet behaviour and attentiveness of the Hottentots. We learned from them that we were to expect many more visitors from the Cape; which, however, was no pleasant news to us: for the visitors from there always stay overnight with us, and have a great many slaves, men and women, in their train; so that we often do not know how we are to get them all under a roof; though the trouble of this has been somewhat eased by the two rooms that we have built on to our church.

<!-- lines 15530-15545 -->
The rain, which went on for some days, not only hindered us in our work on a kraal for our cattle, but pieces of the newly plastered wall fell off again as well. We would indeed have wished to keep dry weather without a break until this work was finished: for whether work is done or not, [...] help, and such people often make up a number of 20 and more, goes on all the same [4]. Nevertheless we rejoiced greatly over this rain, which came so just at the right time for the corn; since otherwise a failed harvest would have been to be feared.

<!-- lines 15546-15547 -->
On the 12th 2 sisters partook of Holy Communion with us for the first time.

<!-- lines 15548-15559 -->
On the 25th we again had a visit from the overseer of the warm bath, together with 3 gentlemen from the Cape, and the next day another wagon came with 6 persons. Baas Teunis also brought a party of 6 persons to us, among whom were 3 sisters of the wife of Mr Rhenius [5]. So we again had 16 guests, all of whom, however, left in the afternoon after attending the Sunday meeting on the 27th.

## Notes

1. *N. XII. A. 1797.*: an archive or registry mark on the cover of this copy, left untranslated. The copy of the next diary in the file has *No 1. B. 1797.* (entry 64). The file holds this diary (5 November 1796 to 19 May 1797) after the first diary of the mission (entries 49 to 56), and the diary of 25 June to 1 November 1796 after it (entries 64 to 68), so the two 1796 diaries stand in the file in reverse order.
2. *(Exempl. 3.) te Bl.*: "copy 3", with a note that may refer to a page (*Bl.*, *Blad*) or a place. The German column has *zu Bl.* The meaning is not clear. To be checked against the image.
3. *de Hr Wolfram*: the neighbour called Wolfrum in entry 33 (February 1796); see Wolfrum in the glossary. The overseer of the warm bath is not named here; in July 1796 he is Negrini (entry 65).
4. Line 15538 is missing, one of the every-82nd-line gaps. The transcription runs *want, 't zy er gewerkt wordt of niet,* / *helpen, en de zulken maaken dikwyls een getal van 20, en nog meer uit, gaat zynen gang.* The lost line probably said that the feeding or keeping of the Khoekhoe who come to help goes on whether or not work can be done; there is no other copy to settle it.
5. *de Vrouw van den Heer Renius*: probably the wife of Johan Isaac Rhenius, the former commander at Swellendam (see Rhenius in the glossary). Not certain.
