On 9 August Brother Kühnel made the journey to the farmer Redlichhausen, who has got us some sheep. He had made a journey of 3½ months into the land of the Caffers to buy oxen and sheep [1]; and now we have a small flock of the latter, which, though we had spared no pains, we had not been able to manage before. On the 13th we celebrated with some solemn meetings, among other things with the administering of baptism to 2 elderly women, and with the enjoyment of Holy Communion [2]. On the 25th, with the help of a good 10 Hottentots, we got our sheep, 200 in number, safely across the river, which had been much swollen in the preceding days [3]. Some of them had lambs already. On the 26th we also got our new garden, or rather vineyard, in order. We have planted 2000 vines in the hope that they will bear fruit after [3 years have passed. At] this time too we took much trouble to get millstones, and looked for them in many places, but in vain [4].

<!-- lines 12427-12438 -->
On the 29th we celebrated our choir festival, and read among other things a choir address that Brother Reichel had given to the choir of single brothers at Herrnhut in the year 1795, and which served all the more to rouse us, since mention was made of us in it twice [5]. In our morning blessing we also remembered Brother and Sister Kohrhammer, as we also do every Saturday in our evening meetings, since we have heard that they have been called here [6].

## Notes

1. *tot in het land der Caffers*: the country of the Xhosa. Redlichhaüser is Redlichhausen; see the glossary.
2. *Den 13.*; the second copy (line 13157) has "the 18th". The 13th of August is a Moravian festival day, the memorial of the renewal of the Unity in 1727, which supports the reading of the first copy.
3. *met hulpe van wel los Hottentotten*; the second copy (line 13164) has *wel 10 Hottentotten*, which is followed here.
4. Line 12422 is missing. The transcription runs *op hoop, dat dezelven na verloop van* / *deezen tijd gaven wij ons ook veel moeite*. The second copy (lines 13171 to 13173) has *na verloop van 3 jaar vrucht zullen draagen. In deezen tÿd*; the words in square brackets are supplied from there. The second copy has 2600 vines (line 13170).
5. The single brothers' choir festival was kept on 29 August; see Chor in the glossary. Brother Reichel is not identified. The three missionaries were single brothers.
6. *aan Br: J. Z. Kohrhammer*: the second copy (line 13185) has *Br: en Zr: Kohrhammer*; compare entry 36, note 5.
