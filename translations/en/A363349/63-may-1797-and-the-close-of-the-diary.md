On 17 May, to our great joy, we received letters and congregation reports from Europe through Baas Teunis, whose son had been at the Cape. Among the latter we found some translated into Dutch, which was especially welcome to us. A chest of books for us had been left standing at Baas Teunis's. To fetch it we borrowed some oxen from the Hottentots, because ours, since we have had to use them so much for our building, had grown so thin that we cannot make use of them for the present. We have only 7 head, and we must yoke at least 6 to a cart. When one has loaded only 3 schepels of corn, a load of about 500 pounds, 8 oxen are needed for it [1].

<!-- lines 16098-16132 -->
On the 18th, when some children had not behaved quietly or orderly in the school, and a beginning was now also to be made with teaching them the first principles of religion, Brother Kühnel said to them that he had no liberty to speak of the Saviour with such children; for they showed by their behaviour that they did not care about the Saviour: so they might just as well go home. [...] among the children [2]. They fell upon Brother Kühnel [...] and wherever they could reach him, and begged that he would forgive them, and not drive them away: they would pray the Saviour to give them obedient hearts: for they did wish to become children of the Saviour, and to give him their hearts, and so on. At this Brother Kühnel could not keep from weeping; for the praying and weeping of the children was very strong. All the same he let them go home this time, with the admonition that they should first pray the Saviour for forgiveness; then all would be well again. In the evening most of them came, and asked very much to be allowed to attend the singing meeting: which was granted them.

<!-- lines 16133-16145 -->
The next day, the 19th, they came to school early, and first asked forgiveness for their disobedience, and promised to offer themselves wholly to the Saviour from now on. Each child wanted to make its request and promise separately as well; which, however, could not be done because of their number [3]. Brother Kühnel then took them together, and spoke with them lovingly and earnestly, and assured them that he had forgiven them [4].

<!-- lines 16146-16159 -->
With this we close our present diary, and commend ourselves to the prayers and remembrance of our dear brothers and sisters before the Saviour, and likewise our dear congregation from among the Hottentots, which at present consists of:

17 communicants
9 candidates for Communion
26 baptised adults
10 baptised children, and
53 candidates for baptism, in all

112 persons [5]. Two excluded persons are not counted.
