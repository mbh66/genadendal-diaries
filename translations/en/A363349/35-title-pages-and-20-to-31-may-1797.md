Reports of the mission among the Hottentots, from 20 May 1797 to 28 February 1798 [1].

<!-- lines 12208-12212 -->
Reports from the diary of the missionaries among the Hottentots at Baviaanskloof, from 20 May to 31 October 1797.

<!-- lines 12213-12223 -->
On the 20th a sick Hottentot sent for Brother Kühnel [2]. He complained to him that he had violent stitches in his side: but, he added, when I think of the stitch that the Saviour suffered for my sake, my pain grows less. He also asked very urgently to be accepted among the candidates for baptism. "I am not worthy of it indeed," he said, "of that I am convinced; but I long for it very strongly."

<!-- lines 12224-12245 -->
In the evening, in the meeting of the baptised, we gave them the greetings of our dear brothers and sisters in England [3], and told them how much all the congregations shared in their flourishing and growth, and how it was wished for them, among other things, that they might be united in fellowship of the spirit with the other congregations from among the heathen; and that for this reason accounts had now been sent to them as well, which we meant to read to them from time to time. Accordingly we made a start with an account from Greenland of the year 1777. It struck us that we had received these accounts just at the present time, since we had come to the end of reading the *Idea fidei fratrum*, and did not well know what we should read now.

<!-- lines 12246-12261 -->
On the 25th we celebrated Ascension Day. In the morning we held a morning blessing with the baptised and the candidates for baptism, and afterwards two general meetings. Many came and asked very earnestly to be accepted among the candidates for baptism; as indeed, in general, hardly a day goes by without such requests being made to us. On the 31st we spoke with the baptised, on which occasion we [could rejoice] over most of them, [especially over the com]municants, with whom we enjoyed Holy Communion in the evening [4]. Elisabeth looked on at it for the second time.
