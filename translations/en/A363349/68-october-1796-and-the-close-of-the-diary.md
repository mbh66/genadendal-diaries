Now since the dry summer season begins in October, we decided to build a kraal for our cattle and a workshop for Brother Kühnel. On the 12th we heard from Baas Teunis that an order had come from the General that nobody might fell wood without permission; which therefore caused delay in our work.

<!-- lines 16681-16692 -->
Further, we can still rejoice over our candidates for baptism, who all have the desire to become the property of Jesus; with their ignorance we must certainly have some patience yet. [...] to be [...] is still very great [1]. Every day some come, indeed sometimes many, and declare this with tears. We commend them and ourselves to the remembrance and prayers of all our brothers and sisters.

## Notes

1. Line 16686 is missing, the last of the every-82nd-line gaps in the file. The transcription runs *moeten wij zeker nog wat geduld hebben.* / *te worden nog eer groot.* The lost line probably spoke of the longing of many (perhaps of those not yet candidates) to be saved (*zalig te worden*), and *eer* is probably a misreading of *zeer*, "very". The words in the translation are only those that survive. The diary ends here, with no date, figures or signatures; the next diary (entry 57) begins on 5 November 1796.
2. Lines 16693 to 16696 are the markers of pages 277a to 278b, which are nearly empty, and line 16699 is the marker of page 279. Archief Z.Z.G., inv. 1146.*, are in modern Dutch: an archive label for the file or this part of it, not part of the diary. *Z.Z.G.* is probably the Zeister Zendingsgenootschap, the Moravian mission society at Zeist.
