On 7 August we received the sad news that one of our schoolboys, who was going to the Cape with a farmer as leader of the oxen, had the misfortune on the way to fall from the wagon; the wheels went over his body and he died on the spot.

<!-- lines 326-337 -->
On the 8th a 3-year-old girl whom we had baptised fell asleep. A few days before her end she said to her mother: "I am not afraid of death, for then I shall go to the dear Saviour, and he will surely take me in." Another time she said: "Don't hold me back, for I must see the dear Saviour himself, and he will surely come soon and call me to him."

<!-- lines 337-351 -->
On 13 August 5 adults and 2 children were baptised into the death of Jesus. When we asked them about their intentions, all of them had said that it was the wish of their hearts to live and die for the Saviour. In the evening we partook of the body and blood of the Lord in Holy Communion, a grace that two persons shared with us for the first time. Three were present as confirmands and 5 as candidates.

<!-- lines 352-401 -->
On the 18th we had a very unpleasant scene. Three months earlier our herdsmen had found a strange horse on our fields. We gave it to a Hottentot, a brother named Petrus Blesier, to look after until the owner came forward. At the same time, as the law of the land here requires, we reported to the Landdrost in Stellenbosch that a strange horse was with us. Soon afterwards we received a letter from a farmer who, with many harsh words, accused the said Petrus Blesier of stealing his horse. We sent word that he should come and fetch his horse himself, and that we would then speak with him. Now he came, together with a field corporal, and once he had properly proved that he owned the horse, it was handed over to him at once. But then we demanded that he declare the Hottentot Petrus Blesier an honest man and admit that he himself had written what was untrue. At first he would not agree, because, he said, it was only a Hottentot and no Christian. But we told him that if he did not give this declaration in writing we would know what to do, and that he would find out that the laws of the land protect the innocent. So at last he had to comply, and we were very glad to see the matter settled this way without further trouble.

<!-- lines 401-415 -->
It is a very common thing for the farmers to allow themselves such unjust accusations against the Hottentots: whenever thefts or other bad things happen on a farm, a Hottentot must always be the culprit. We cannot help taking the part of our people in such cases, just as, on the other hand, we are very strict with all who really are guilty of such things, and send them away from us as the case requires.

After very violent thunderstorms on 23 August, we had very cold and wet weather in the last days of this month.
